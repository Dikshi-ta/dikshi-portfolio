Smart LifeLink — Organ Transplant Platform

An AI-powered organ transplant management platform designed to connect donors, recipients, administrators, and transport teams through a centralized digital healthcare workflow.

1. Project Overview

Smart LifeLink is an organ transplant management platform designed to organize and simplify the different stages of the transplant coordination process.

The platform brings together donor information, recipient requirements, organ matching, administrative management, and organ transportation into a connected digital workflow.

The goal of the project is to provide a centralized platform where authorized users can manage important transplant-related information efficiently.

2. Problem Statement

Organ transplantation involves multiple participants and requires coordination between donors, recipients, medical teams, administrators, and transportation teams.

Managing this information through disconnected systems can make the workflow difficult to organize and monitor.

Smart LifeLink explores a centralized platform that can organize transplant information and provide visibility across different stages of the workflow.

3. Project Objectives

  • Create a centralized organ transplant management platform.
  • Manage donor and recipient information.
  • Support organ matching between suitable donors and recipients.
  • Provide an organized administrative dashboard.
  • Monitor organ transportation.
  • Maintain organized records and audit information.
  • Create a clear and user-friendly healthcare interface.

4. Main Features

  • Donor Management: Stores and manages donor-related information within the platform.
  • Recipient Management: Organizes recipient requirements and transplant-related information.
  • AI-Based Matching: Explores intelligent matching between donor organs and recipient requirements.
  • Transport Tracking: Provides a workflow for monitoring the movement of organs during transportation.
  • Admin Dashboard: Allows authorized administrators to manage platform information and activities.
  • Audit and Records: Organizes important activity and transaction records for traceability.
  • Healthcare Workflow: Connects different users and stages of the transplant management process.

6. How the Platform Works

  1. Donor information is registered in the platform.
  2. Available organ information is recorded.
  3. Recipient information and requirements are entered into the system.
  4. The matching system analyzes relevant donor and recipient information.
  5. Potential matches can be reviewed by authorized users.
  6. The transplant coordination workflow proceeds according to the required process.
  7. Transportation information can be monitored.
  8. Relevant activities can be recorded for administrative and audit purposes.

7. System Workflow

The platform connects donor management, recipient management, matching, administration, and transportation into a single workflow.

8. Design and User Experience

The Smart LifeLink interface is designed around clarity, structured information, and easy navigation.

Healthcare-related information can contain multiple data points, so the interface uses organized dashboards, cards, tables, and status indicators to make information easier to understand.

Different user roles are separated so that relevant information and actions can be presented according to the user's responsibilities.

9. Design Process

The design process involved understanding the transplant-management workflow, defining the major user roles, organizing information architecture, creating interface layouts, designing dashboards, and refining the overall user experience.
